## What it does

`GraspProbePolicy(schema, observation_joint_names, defaults, dt=.02, *, settle_calls, ramp_calls, reach_only, forward_cmd, turn_cap, standoff, turn_gain, lowering_m)` — rejects `reach_only=True` and any `lowering_m != .02`, and composes a private `FirstReachPolicy` built from the caller's own parameters (`task_b/grasp_probe.py:138`). It exposes `act(proprio84, images)`, `state`, `done_reason`, `debug`, `alpha`, `pause_for_stance` (property forwarding to the child), `describe()` and `wheel_hold_requested`.

- **Prefix**: exactly one inner call per step, action returned verbatim; any inner reason other than `reach_lowering_hold_complete` becomes the wrapper's `done_reason` unchanged. `wheel_hold_requested` mirrors the evaluator's fallback state test during the prefix, then stays true for every remaining call, so the anchor is engaged continuously and never re-anchored. The leg slice in probe actions is read off the child's own latched `lowering_alpha * lower_delta`, so the achieved lowered reference cannot move; wheels stay zero and no locomotion is commanded.
- **PROBE_CLOSE** (≤3 s): six joints pinned to the child's achieved hold command; fingers slew from the previous actual command at ≤.05 m/s to `gripper_targets(0, preload_m=.015)` (−.015/+.015 m) through `arm_targets_to_action` with original defaults/scale/order; needs ≥1.4 s active plus 10 consecutive width samples with range <.002 m and median in [.006,.065] m; `grasp_probe_empty_close` / `grasp_probe_close_not_confirmed` otherwise. No retry or re-squeeze.
- **PROBE_LIFT** (≤5 s): measured q latched once, `q2−.10`, `q3+.10`, others unchanged, checked against the chain limits intersected with the live soft limits — a violation stops with `grasp_probe_lift_goal_outside_joint_limits` rather than clipping. Motion at ≤.10 rad/s per axis with a ±.10 rad measured tether, closed finger target retained; arrival needs error <.04 rad, arm qdot <.05 rad/s and body-frame FK pinch rise along public up ≥.015 m held .3 s. `no_joint_lift_progress`, `grasp_probe_lift_not_reached`, `grasp_probe_empty_during_lift`.
- **PROBE_OBSERVE** (2 s): same goal, target, leg reference, anchor and bounds; normal end `grasp_probe_observation_complete`, documented in `STOP_REASONS` and `describe()` as "the observation phase ran" and explicitly not a grasp/lift/transport/score claim.
- Bounds: unchanged .06/.12 pause thresholds with .2 s quiet resume, 2 s per episode and 2 s cumulative (explicit reasons); .25 hard tilt, .12/.45 instability; entry-anchored .03 m/.05 rad/.05 rad twist budgets that keep integrating through pauses; 10 s total from entry; immediate stop on non-84/non-finite proprio. Pauses reset only the width/arrival/observation windows.
- Diagnostics merge the child's `debug` (child values also kept under `first_reach_*`) and add phase, entry call, elapsed/remaining, pause counters, held command, q7/q8/width window with range and median, finger targets, latched q, paired delta, max q error/qdot, pinch displacement, budgets, `wheel_hold_requested` and the labelled fixed-candidate constants.
