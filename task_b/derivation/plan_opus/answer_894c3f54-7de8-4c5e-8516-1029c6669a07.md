## Caveats
- Detector mock now records the `locked_target_body` kwarg (still `**kwargs`-tolerant); it ignores the lock, so policy-level lock behavior is audited via plumbing + the real-detector synthetic cases, not by mock filtering.
- `missing_vision_does_not_disable_total_reach_deadline` shortens `reach_timeout_s` on the instance to exercise the production guard quickly.
- Synthetic frames only prove filter arithmetic; they are not evidence that the real overhead component will be re-associated in the original env.
- Budget integration is dead-reckoning of public twist (no world truth); a slow drift below the 50 Hz noise floor is not detectable by it, which is why the instantaneous stop remains.
- With `lowering_m == 0` the strict `reach_target_moved_beyond_12cm` exit can no longer fire (by design); with the locked detector active, distant components are also filtered before the policy sees them.
